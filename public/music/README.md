# Music bed

Drop licensed loops in here (any `.mp3` / `.wav` / `.m4a` / `.ogg`). Every
file becomes part of a rotation: each video deterministically picks one by
trade id, mixed under the voiceover at volume 0.1 (set in
`scripts/voiceover.mjs`, `music.volume`). Tracks must be cleared for
commercial social-media use; with an empty folder videos render with
narration only.

Good free libraries (check each license for commercial social use):
Pixabay Music, Mixkit, Uppbeat (attribution on the free tier),
Free Music Archive (per-track CC terms).
